Interest coverage and cash support near-term financial resilience, but Debt/EBITDA is not testable because complete D&A is missing. No untestable rule is reported as passed. Decision drivers:

- Recurring operating cash and interest coverage support survival, while complete EBITDA is unavailable. Change condition: A liquidity shortfall, materially higher financing costs or lower cash conversion would weaken this assessment.
- Reported earnings include volatile investment gains. Change condition: Use recurring operating profitability and cash conversion; do not capitalize the gains as repeatable earnings.
